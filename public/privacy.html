Privacy Policy

DocGen AI — last updated 23 September 2026

This policy explains what DocGen AI ("the app", "we") collects when you use the website or the Android app, and why. Questions: ulugbekisoqov22@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and profile picture URL. If you use an email and password instead, the password is stored only as a bcrypt hash.
  • Chats and documents — the messages you send and the documents you create or edit are stored in our database, tied to your account, so they are available on all your devices. Attached files and images are sent to the AI provider to answer your request but are not stored; only the file name is kept for your chat history.
  • Reports — if you report an AI response, we keep your report and a copy of the reported content so we can review it.
  • Usage data — how many documents you created in the current 30-day period, so plan limits can be applied, and the time of your last sign-in.
  • Payment information — if you buy Pro on our website, payment is handled entirely by the payment provider (Paddle worldwide; Payme or Click in Uzbekistan). We never see or store card numbers. We store your plan, its end date and the provider's transaction reference.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or rent your data.

Services that process your data

  • DeepSeek — the AI model provider. It receives the text and images of your requests (and the document being edited) to generate a response. See DeepSeek's privacy policy.
  • Google — if you choose "Continue with Google", Google confirms your identity and shares your name, email and profile picture with us.
  • Paddle, Payme and Click — receive what they need to process a payment if you buy Pro on the website.
  • Render — our hosting provider, which runs the server and database.

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

On your device

Your sign-in token and preferences (theme, preferred file format) are kept in the app's local storage. Files you export are created on your device and saved only where you choose.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any chat or document at any time from the side menu.
  • Delete your whole account in the app: Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, chats and documents, and cancels an active card subscription.
  • Can't access the app? See how to request account deletion.

Payment records (amount, date, provider reference — no card data) are kept, unlinked from your account, for as long as tax law requires.

Children

DocGen AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
